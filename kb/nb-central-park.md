Central Park is the current name of the neighborhood that used to be called Stapleton. Use Central Park in searches and notes. Houses here are mostly 2000s builds with two-car garages and small HOA dues for landscaping. A three-bedroom in this catalog is $615k. The old name still appears in casual speech; the listing address uses Central Park.
