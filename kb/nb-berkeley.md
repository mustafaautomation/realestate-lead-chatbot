topic: neighborhood
---

Berkeley is west of the Highlands, around Tennyson Street. Bungalows and small houses are the usual stock, with some duplex condos. Yards are more common than in LoHi. In this catalog a three-bedroom house can still appear near $600k. Tennyson is a commercial strip; the guide talks about the houses, not about the people who shop there.
