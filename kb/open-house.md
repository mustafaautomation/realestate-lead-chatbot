Open houses are advertised to the general public, not to a private list. They run Saturday 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM or Sunday 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM, and only when the listing shows that window. Sign-in is requested and is not required. A Summit Ridge agent stays on site the whole time. There are no unattended lockbox tours during the public window. Private showings are not stacked on top of the open house. Food is prepackaged. No alcohol. The open house is advertised to the general public. It is not limited to a private list, a club, or a group of people. Fair housing rules apply at the door the same way they apply in this chat. A private showing outside those windows is booked with schedule_showing.
