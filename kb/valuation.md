estimate_home_value is a mock AVM. It multiplies finished square feet by a price-per-foot figure for the neighborhood, then widens a range of 8 percent on either side. It is not an appraisal, not a Zestimate, and not a promise of what a buyer will pay. It ignores condition, lot, view, and renovations except for a small bedroom adjustment. A seller who wants a real number books a listing consult with Elena Voss and walks the house. Give the desk an address, bedroom count, and square footage and it will run the formula. If the address does not name a known neighborhood, the formula uses a Denver fallback of $400 a foot and says so.
