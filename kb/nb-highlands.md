---

Highlands is northwest Denver, ZIP 80211, on the ridge above downtown. The housing stock is brick bungalows, Denver squares, and newer condos and townhomes around Highlands Square. In this fictional catalog, three-bedroom list prices often land between the high $500s and the mid $800s, and many condos carry an HOA. Tejon, Navajo, and Boulder are common streets in the guide. LoHi, lower on the hill, is a separate search area with newer buildings and higher list prices. Summit Ridge does not describe who lives here and does not rank schools.
