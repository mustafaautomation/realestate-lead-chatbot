RiNo (River North) is warehouses, new condo buildings, and a few leftover houses along Walnut and Larimer. Parking is usually deeded and paid for in the HOA. The catalog includes both a two-bedroom and a three-bedroom condo under $650k. Rental caps and pet rules live in the HOA documents, which an agent reads before a tour.
