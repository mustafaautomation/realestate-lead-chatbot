title: Neighborhood guide: Sloan's Lake
topic: neighborhood
---

Sloan's Lake is the neighborhood on the east side of the lake and park path. Houses are mostly early bungalows plus a handful of newer builds. A lake path is the amenity the guide will mention. This catalog's three-bedroom houses start in the high $600s. We do not rate the park by who uses it.
