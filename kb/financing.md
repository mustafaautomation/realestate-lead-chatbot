This desk explains financing. It does not quote a rate, a payment, or an APR, and it is not a loan offer. Pre-qualification is a conversation with a lender about income and a stated price. Pre-approval is a lender's conditional letter after a credit pull and document check, and it is what listing agents expect with an offer. That comparison is educational and is not a loan offer. Cash means no loan contingency. Conventional loans often ask for a down payment somewhere between 5 and 20 percent. FHA programs can start near 3.5 percent down for eligible buyers. VA loans can be zero down for eligible veterans. Those are program descriptions, not terms Summit Ridge can lock. A licensed loan officer is the person who quotes a rate. If you are pre-approved, say so and the lead score treats it as a strength. If the letter is still in process, say that too.
