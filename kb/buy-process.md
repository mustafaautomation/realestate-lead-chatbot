Summit Ridge's buyer path is eight steps. One: a conversation that records budget, pre-approval or cash, timeline, and what prompted the move. Two: a written buyer agreement before the first private showing, including how the broker is paid. Three: a search of this catalog by price, bedrooms, area, and property type. Four: private showings or a public open house. Five: an offer with earnest money, inspection, and appraisal deadlines a Colorado contract would normally carry. Six: inspection and, if there is a loan, appraisal. Seven: a title commitment and a final walkthrough. Eight: closing at a local title company. Nothing on this list is legal advice. The demo does not write a real contract.
