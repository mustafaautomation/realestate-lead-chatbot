Golden is a separate city west of Denver, at the edge of the foothills. Summit Ridge's west-metro agent covers it. The catalog has a house near downtown Golden at $780k and a townhome at $649k. Commute into downtown Denver is longer than from Highlands. That is a drive-time fact, not a comment about the town.
