LoHi (Lower Highlands) sits below the Highlands ridge, closer to the pedestrian bridge and the downtown grid. The catalog leans toward condos and townhomes built after 2000, with HOA dues and deeded parking. List prices in this demo run higher than Highlands for a similar bedroom count. Search LoHi on its own; it is not folded into a Highlands search.
