A seller starts with a valuation visit, not a listing agreement. Elena Voss brings a mock AVM range and a net sheet that uses the published fee schedule. The seller chooses price, prep, and whether to list. Prep is usually paint, a pre-inspection, and a weekend of photos. The listing goes on the demo catalog with an open-house window if the seller wants one. Offers are compared on price, concessions, and dates, not on who the buyer is. Under contract, the seller completes the inspection objection and then closes. This is a fictional process for the portfolio demo.
