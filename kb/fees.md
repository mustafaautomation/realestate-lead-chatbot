What a seller pays: the published listing-side schedule is 5.5 percent of the sale price. This synthetic fee schedule is for a fictional brokerage. It is not a quote and not a legal document. The 5.5 percent is customarily split evenly with a cooperating buyer broker, 2.75 percent and 2.75 percent. The split is negotiable and is written into the listing agreement before the house is advertised. Summit Ridge does not charge buyer clients a separate admin fee. Buyer-broker compensation is agreed in writing before the first private tour, and it may be paid by the seller, the buyer, or a combination. There is no fee to ask a question in this chat, and a valuation range from the mock AVM is free. A net sheet subtracts the 5.5 percent schedule, a title estimate the seller's agent fills in, and any concessions. Change any of those numbers only by editing the published schedule, not in the chat.
